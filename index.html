About this edition

A reading edition of the qunūt: Arabic, transliteration, English and German — transcribed from Mohammed Sadat Abdulai's verified 2012 recitation transliteration.

AI-assisted Arabic & translation The transliteration's wording is the author's own, verified 2012 recitation transcript. The Arabic script, its diacritics, and the English and German translations here were reconstructed and translated with AI assistance and have not yet been checked by a qualified Arabic reader. Treat them as a careful draft, not a final text — see REVIEW.md for specifics.

This project presents Mohammed Sadat Abdulai's own, verified transliteration of the qunūt recited by Shaykh ʿAbdallāh Muḥammad al-Maṭrūd, in a clear, phone-friendly reading edition, alongside Arabic script and English and German translations.

The transliteration follows the same academic scheme (macrons, ʿayn/hamza marks, il-/ul- assimilation) used in the companion al-Wird al-Laṭīf edition. The Arabic and the translations are a first, AI-assisted pass awaiting a qualified reader's check — see REVIEW.md for exactly which lines are least certain.

Hausa and Dagbanli translations are planned but not yet written.

Prepared by Mohammed Sadat Abdulai
